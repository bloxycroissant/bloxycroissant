## 🚀 Featured Group Projects & Leadership

### 🌍 1. Biosphere Quest *(Mobile Computing Group Project)*
> **Role:** 👑 Project Owner, Team Leader & Lead Developer
>
> A gamified mobile educational application for Grades 1–6 (aligned with the MATATAG curriculum). Features space exploration with mascots **Astro & Stella**, interactive math/science quizzes, an arcade **"Math Ninja" slicing mini-game**, and a **200+ item avatar customizer shop** featuring legendary gear like the **Valkyrie Helm** and **Dominus Astra**.
>
> 🛠️ **Tech Stack:** `React Native` • `Expo` • `TypeScript` • `Reanimated` • `Gesture Handler` • `AsyncStorage` • `Supabase`

### 🥗 2. MacroMate *(Object-Oriented Programming Group Project)*
> **Role:** 👑 Team Leader & Lead UI/UX Designer
>
> A comprehensive wellness tracker and nutrition journal designed to help users log meals, monitor macronutrients, and build healthy lifestyle habits through a polished, intuitive interface.
>
> 🛠️ **Tech Stack:** `Java` • `React` • `Vite` • `TypeScript` • `Tailwind CSS` • `PostgreSQL`
